I am Zhaohui Chen, a researcher at Alibaba Group. I received my Ph.D. in Information Security from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences and conducted postdoctoral research in Computer Science and Technology at Peking University. My work focuses on hardware security, cryptographic engineering, and privacy-preserving computation.

My publications cover efficient hardware for post-quantum cryptography, homomorphic encryption accelerators, and privacy-preserving AI systems. See my [publications](/publications/) for details.
