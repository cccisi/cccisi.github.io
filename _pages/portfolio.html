--- layout: archive title: "Open Proposal" permalink: /portfolio/ author_profile: true --- {% include base_path %} ## Continuous Model Identity Attestation for Distributed LLM Serving An open research proposal on inference-time verification of deployed LLM weights, adapters, and selected shared cache state. The two-page PDF describes the threat model, system design, evaluation plan, and limits of the proposed guarantees. **[Download the research proposal (PDF)](/files/continuous-model-identity-attestation.pdf)** The core research directions and this proposal originate with **Zhaohui Chen (2026)**. The proposed prototype is intended for release under **Apache License 2.0** with attribution in its NOTICE file; the proposal text is shared under **CC BY 4.0** with attribution. Research and publications based on this proposal are invited **in collaboration with Zhaohui Chen**.
